[DOC: Frequently Asked Questions]

Q: How do I apply for a duplicate ID card?
A: Submit an application at the Administrative Office with a copy of your Aadhar/passport and a payment of INR 200. Duplicate cards are issued within 3 working days.

Q: How can I get a bonafide certificate?
A: Bonafide certificates can be requested through the Student Portal under "Certificate Requests." Processing takes 2-4 working days and the certificate can be collected from the Registrar's office or downloaded digitally once approved.

Q: Where can I check my exam results?
A: Results are published on the Student Portal under the "Examinations" tab. You will also receive an email notification once results are declared.

Q: How do I apply for a transcript?
A: Transcript requests are made through the Registrar's office portal. Official transcripts take 5-7 working days to process and cost INR 500 per copy; a rush service (2 days) is available for INR 1000.

Q: Can I change my elective course after the semester starts?
A: Elective changes are allowed only within the first 2 weeks of the semester, subject to seat availability, and require approval from both the current and new course coordinators.

Q: What is the process for hostel room allotment?
A: Hostel allotment is done through the Hostel Portal during the admission/re-registration period. Rooms are allotted based on seniority and availability; first-year students are allotted rooms centrally by the hostel office.

Q: How do I reset my student portal password?
A: Click "Forgot Password" on the login page and follow the OTP verification sent to your registered email or phone number. If you no longer have access to these, visit the IT Helpdesk in the Admin Building with your ID card.

Q: Who do I contact for scholarship-related queries?
A: The Financial Aid Office, located in the Administrative Block Room 108, handles all scholarship and financial aid queries. Email: finaid@college.edu

Q: How do I join a student club or society?
A: Club registrations open during the first two weeks of each semester. Visit the Student Activity Center or check the Clubs tab on the Student Portal to sign up.

Q: What should I do if I lose my library book?
A: Report the loss immediately to the Library Help Desk. You will be charged the replacement cost of the book plus a processing fee of INR 100.

Q: How many days of sick leave are allowed without a medical certificate?
A: Up to 2 consecutive days of absence can be self-certified. Absences beyond 2 days require a medical certificate from a registered practitioner to be eligible for attendance condonation.

Q: Is there a grievance redressal mechanism?
A: Yes, students can submit grievances through the online Grievance Redressal Portal or approach the Student Grievance Committee, which meets every two weeks. Anonymous complaints are also accepted for serious concerns.

Q: How do I apply for an internship through the college?
A: Internship opportunities are posted on the Placement Cell portal. Students must have a minimum CGPA of 6.0 and no active backlogs to apply through college-facilitated internship drives.
