SECTION 1: ATTENDANCE POLICY
Students must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in each course to be eligible to sit for the end-semester examination. Students with attendance between 65% and 75% may be granted condonation by the Head of Department only on medical or genuine grounds, supported by valid documentation submitted within 7 days of resuming classes. Students below 65% attendance will not be permitted to appear for the semester exam and must repeat the course.

SECTION 2: GRADING SYSTEM
The college follows a 10-point Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) system.
Grade O = 10 points (90-100 marks)
Grade A+ = 9 points (80-89 marks)
Grade A = 8 points (70-79 marks)
Grade B+ = 7 points (60-69 marks)
Grade B = 6 points (50-59 marks)
Grade C = 5 points (40-49 marks)
Grade F = 0 points (Below 40 marks, Fail)
A minimum CGPA of 5.0 is required to remain in good academic standing. Students falling below this threshold for two consecutive semesters will be placed on academic probation.

SECTION 3: EXAMINATION RULES
Students must carry their ID card to every examination. Use of mobile phones or smart devices in the exam hall is strictly prohibited and will be treated as a malpractice case. Any student found using unfair means during an examination will have their answer script cancelled and will be reported to the Disciplinary Committee. Re-evaluation requests must be submitted within 10 days of result declaration along with the prescribed fee.

SECTION 4: MAKEUP EXAMINATIONS
Students who miss an end-semester exam due to medical emergencies, family emergencies, or participation in college-approved events may apply for a makeup examination. Applications must be submitted to the Examination Cell within 5 working days of the missed exam, along with supporting documents. Makeup exams are generally scheduled within 2-3 weeks after the regular exam period.

SECTION 5: BACKLOG AND REVALUATION
A student who fails a course (backlog) must re-register for it in a subsequent semester when it is offered, or through the special backlog exam window offered in December and June. A maximum of 4 backlog courses may be carried at any point; students exceeding this limit will not be promoted to the next year.

SECTION 6: LEAVE OF ABSENCE
Students requiring an extended leave of absence (more than 2 weeks) must apply through the Dean of Student Affairs office with appropriate justification. Leave beyond 30 days in a semester may require the student to withdraw and re-register for the semester in a later term.

SECTION 7: CODE OF CONDUCT
Ragging in any form is strictly prohibited and punishable under the Anti-Ragging Act, including suspension or expulsion. Plagiarism in assignments, projects, or theses will result in a zero for that submission on the first offense and potential disciplinary action for repeat offenses. All plagiarism cases are reviewed by the Academic Integrity Committee.

SECTION 8: FEE PAYMENT AND REFUND
Tuition fees must be paid within the first 2 weeks of each semester. A late fee penalty of 2% per week applies after the deadline. Students withdrawing within the first month of a semester are eligible for a 50% refund of tuition fees; no refunds are issued after the first month.

SECTION 9: HOSTEL RULES
Hostel curfew timing is 10:00 PM on weekdays and 11:30 PM on weekends. Overnight outings require prior written permission from the warden and parent/guardian confirmation. Ragging, possession of prohibited substances, or unauthorized guests in hostel rooms are grounds for immediate disciplinary action, including expulsion from the hostel.

SECTION 10: SCHOLARSHIPS AND FINANCIAL AID
Merit scholarships are awarded to students scoring a CGPA of 9.0 or above with no active backlogs, covering up to 50% of tuition. Need-based financial aid applications are reviewed each semester by the Financial Aid Committee; students must submit income certificates and the aid application form by the 15th of the admission month.
